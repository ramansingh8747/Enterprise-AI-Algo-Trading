2. Admin Dashboard & Enterprise Capabilities

  • 🛡️ Dynamic Risk Dashboard (/admin/risk): Real-time Exposure meter, Daily Loss Limit breach alerts, Order Velocity throttler, and Paper Account Reset (₹10,000 to ₹1,00,000 presets).
  • 🚨 Master Kill Switch (/admin/kill-switch): Emergency 1-click circuit breaker to immediately halt all active strategies and cancel open limit orders.
  • 🚪 Live Trading Gate (/admin/live-gate): Institutional safety lock requiring paper trading performance certification before real money execution is permitted.
  • 🔗 Multi-Broker Manager (/admin/brokers): Zerodha Kite, Angel One, Upstox, Dhan, Fyers, and Simulated Paper Sandbox broker integrations.
  • ⚖️ Reconciliation (/admin/reconciliation): Real-time exchange vs. local database trade state mismatch detection and synchronization.
  • 📋 Audit Trail (/admin/audit): Immutable event logging for compliance and governance tracking.

3. Trader Capabilities & Autopilot Strategy Engine

  • ⚡ 132 Quant Strategies Autopilot (/strategies): Rule-Based, ML Classification, FinBERT NLP Sentiment, Pairs Arbitrage, Mean-Reversion, and Momentum Scalping.
  • 🎛️ Dual-Action Individual Card Controls:
    • When Strategy is Stopped: Displays ▶️ Resume Strategy (Green). Clicking it immediately turns the strategy 🟢 Active and renders ⏹️ Stop Strategy (Red).
    • When Strategy is Active: Displays ⏹️ Stop Strategy (Red). Clicking it immediately stops the strategy and renders ▶️ Resume Strategy (Green).
  • 🚀 Global Batch Actions:
    • 🚀 Deploy All to Paper: Deploys and activates all 132 strategies simultaneously in paper sandbox mode.
    • ⏹️ Stop All: Safely halts all 132 strategies in 1 click.
  • 💰 Dynamic 1% Risk Position Sizing:
    Quantity = min( floor((Cash Balance × 0.01) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss)), floor(Max Capital / Entry Price) )
    Ensures maximum capital loss per trade is strictly capped at 1% (₹100 on ₹10,000 capital).
  • 📦 Portfolio & Orders (/orders, /portfolio): Real-time P&L tracking, trailing stop-losses, and manual square-off controls.
  • 📓 Smart Journal (/journal): Automated trade logging with entry/exit logic and performance analytics.

4. Step-by-Step Daily Trading Workflow (Monday to Friday)

  1. 🌅 Pre-Market (09:00 AM - 09:15 AM):
    • Open http://localhost:5173/strategies
    • Verify System Health status is Green.
    • Click 🚀 Deploy All to Paper (or resume selected top strategies).
  2. 🔔 Live Market Hours (09:15 AM - 03:30 PM):
    • Strategies evaluate live ticks and generate quantitative signals.
    • In Auto-Pilot Mode: System auto-places orders, tracks ATR Trailing SL, and exits at target.
    • In Advisory Mode: System requests trader 1-click confirmation before executing orders.
  3. 🌆 Post-Market (03:30 PM - 04:00 PM):
    • Review Daily Net P&L and Win Rate in /dashboard.
    • Click ⏹️ Stop All to park strategies in standby mode.
    • Review automatically logged trades in /journal.
